# Stage 3: Pricing & Channels

**Goal:** Decide how you will price the offer and which routes you will use to reach customers.

## Part A: Pricing

### Key questions

1. What is the value of the outcome to the customer?
2. What do competitors charge?
3. What are our costs and the minimum margin we need?
4. Will we price per unit, per user, per project, by subscription, or by retainer?
5. Do we need local pricing for different markets or currencies?

### Pricing approaches

| Approach | How it works | Best when |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost-plus** | Cost + target margin | Costs are predictable, little differentiation |
| **Competitor-based** | Set relative to market rates | Crowded market with similar offers |
| **Value-based** | Priced on the value delivered | Strong, provable results |
| **Tiered** | Good / Better / Best packages | Customers have different needs and budgets |

## Part B: Channels

### Common channels

- **Direct sales** (outbound, account-based)
- **Partners and distributors**
- **Agents or resellers** (useful for new geographies)
- **Online** (website, marketplaces, social)
- **Events and communities**
- **Referrals**
