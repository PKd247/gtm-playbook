# Stage 4: Launch Plan

**Goal:** Turn the strategy into dated actions with a clear owner for each one.

## Key questions

1. What is the launch date, and what must be ready before it?
2. Who owns each task?
3. What is our budget?
4. How will we handle early customer feedback?

## Launch phases

| Phase | Timing | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-launch** | 8 to 4 weeks before | Build assets, line up partners, brief the team |
| **Soft launch** | 4 to 1 weeks before | Test with a small group, collect feedback |
| **Launch** | Launch week | Announce, activate channels, start outreach |
| **Post-launch** | 1 to 12 weeks after | Follow up, learn, optimise |
