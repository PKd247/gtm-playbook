# GTM Playbook

*A practical, step-by-step go-to-market framework for market entry and product launches.*

Built from 16+ years of business and brand development experience by [Prem Kumar Loganathan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/premkumarloganathan/), founder of [d247 consulting](https://www.d247consulting.in).

---

## Who this is for

- Founders launching a first product
- Business leaders entering a new market or country
- Marketing and sales teams preparing a new offer
- Consultants and advisors who need a repeatable launch framework

## How to use it

Work through the five stages in order. Each file contains a short explanation, key questions to answer, and a fill-in template. Write your answers directly into copies of the templates.

| Stage | File | Output you will have |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Market Research](01-market-research.md) | A clear view of your market, customers and competitors |
| 2 | [Positioning](02-positioning.md) | A positioning statement and core messages |
| 3 | [Pricing & Channels](03-pricing-and-channels.md) | A pricing approach and a prioritised channel plan |
| 4 | [Launch Plan](04-launch-plan.md) | A dated, owner-assigned launch plan |
| 5 | [Metrics](05-metrics.md) | A scorecard to track and improve results |

## The GTM flow at a glance

```
Research  →  Position  →  Price & Channel  →  Launch  →  Measure & Improve
   ↑                                                              |
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Principles

1. **Customer first.** Start from the problem your buyer has, not from your product's features.
2. **Be specific.** A narrow target market beats a vague broad one.
3. **Test before scaling.** Validate with a small group before committing the full budget.
4. **One owner per task.** Every action needs a name and a date.
5. **Measure and adjust.** A GTM plan is a living document.
