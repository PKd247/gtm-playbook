# Stage 1: Market Research

**Goal:** Understand who you are selling to, how big the opportunity is, and who you are up against.

## Key questions

1. What problem are we solving, and how painful is it for the customer?
2. Who experiences this problem most strongly?
3. How large is the reachable market?
4. How do customers solve this problem today?
5. Who are the main competitors and what are their weaknesses?
6. Are there regulatory, cultural or logistical barriers? *(Especially important for cross-border entry.)*
