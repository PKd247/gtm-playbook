# Stage 2: Positioning

**Goal:** Define why a specific customer should choose you over the alternatives, in one clear sentence.

## Key questions

1. What is the single most important benefit we deliver?
2. What do we do better than anyone else for this customer?
3. What proof do we have (results, testimonials, data)?
4. What do we want customers to remember about us?

## Template: Positioning Statement

> **For** [target customer]
> **who** [need or problem],
> **[Brand / product]** is a [category]
> **that** [key benefit].
> **Unlike** [main alternative],
> **we** [key differentiator].
