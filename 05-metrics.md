# Stage 5: Metrics

**Goal:** Track what matters, learn quickly, and improve the plan.

## Key questions

1. What does success look like at 30, 60 and 90 days?
2. Which numbers tell us whether we are on track?
3. How often will we review them, and who decides what changes?

## Core metrics

| Stage | Metric | Example question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Reach, website visits, enquiries | Are the right people seeing us? |
| **Interest** | Leads, meetings booked | Are they engaging? |
| **Conversion** | Win rate, sales cycle length | Are we closing? |
| **Revenue** | Revenue, average deal size | Is it profitable? |
| **Retention** | Repeat purchase, renewals, referrals | Do customers stay? |
| **Efficiency** | Cost per lead, cost per customer | Are we spending wisely? |

## Review rhythm

- **Weekly:** Check leads, meetings and pipeline with the team.
- **Monthly:** Compare results against targets and adjust channels or messaging.
- **Quarterly:** Review the full GTM plan, pricing and positioning.

## Common signals and responses

| Signal | Possible cause | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Many leads, few meetings | Weak follow-up or poor lead quality | Tighten the ICP and improve outreach |
| Many meetings, few deals | Positioning or pricing mismatch | Revisit Stages 2 and 3 |
| Deals won but high churn | Expectation gap or delivery issues | Improve onboarding and messaging |
| High cost per customer | Wrong channel mix | Shift budget to the best-scoring channels |
